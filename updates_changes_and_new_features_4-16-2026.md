# Bar 4 Bar: Professional Workstation Update (4-16-2026)

## 1. The Neural Ambient Engine (Studio Pro)
We have introduced a revolutionary **Generative Ambient Engine** that replaces static background loops.
- **Neural Synthesis**: Using the Web Audio API, the studio now synthesizes unique musical textures in real-time.
- **Intelligent Tempo Sync**: The engine pulls the **BPM** directly from your track settings to stay on grid.
- **Vibe-Aware Textures**: The sound profile (Lofi, Trap, Cinematic) evolves based on the "Vibe" section of your track DNA.
- **Owner Efficiency**: Zero hosting costs. Patterns are generated via tiny JSON strings and rendered locally.

## 2. Economic Tiering & Monetization
The workstation now clearly distinguishes between a **Visitor (Local Artist)** and a **Professional (Studio Pro)**.

### Local Artist (The Demo Room)
- **Single Station Workspace**: Users are limited to **exactly 1 Album slot**. To start a second project, they must delete their current work or upgrade.
- **Portability Lock**: Bulk Vault Export (.json) and formatted PDF exports are disabled. Work is locked to the specific browser/device.
- **DNA Limit**: Only 2 of the 5 "Creative Questions" are active. AI suggestions will be more generic for free users.
- **Standard Priority Queue**: A mandatory **3-second buffer** added to all AI requests to simulate lower-tier bandwidth.
- **Freestyle Cap**: Recording sessions are limited to **30-second bursts**.

### Studio Pro (The Professional Suite)
- **Unlimited Vault Residency**: Start as many albums and tracks as your project requires.
- **Cloud Residency**: Full cross-device sync. Write in the car, finish in the booth.
- **Master DNA Suite**: All 5 Creative Questions unlocked for precision AI alignment.
- **Collaborator Sharing**: Generate read-only links for A&Rs and producers to view your lyrics in a high-end interface.
- **Revision Vault**: Access the last 10 versions of your track to revert any "Creative Drift."
- **Studio Priority**: Instant, high-bandwidth connection to the Gemini AI engine.
- **Neural Style Depth**: Access to a multi-metric "Artist DNA Breakdown" including vocabulary and rhyme complexity scores.

## 3. UI/UX Refinements
- **Studio Guide Update**: The manual now features dedicated sections on the Ambient Engine and Tiering.
- **Contextual Locking**: "Lock" icons and Pro-upgrade prompts are integrated directly into the workflow (DNA inputs, Export buttons, Freestyle Pad).
- **Hardened Build logic**: Refactored the root layout to ensure stable deployments and 404 handling.
