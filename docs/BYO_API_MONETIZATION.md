
# Bar 4 Bar: BYO-API Monetization Strategy

## 1. The Concept: "The Studio vs. The Fuel"
By allowing users to "Bring Their Own API Key," we are shifting the value proposition from "Selling AI access" to "Selling a Professional Workstation."

### Why this makes you MORE money:
*   **Subscription Gate**: This feature is locked behind the **Studio Pro ($11.99/mo)** tier. Users cannot even use their own key unless they are paying you monthly.
*   **Pure Profit Platform Fee**: Even with their own key, users are still charged **Ink** for every action, but at a **50% Discounted Rate**.
    *   *The Value to User*: "Use your own key to bypass global studio rate limits, get faster response times, and cut your Ink usage in half."
    *   *The Value to Owner*: You incur **zero token costs** for these users. Every Ink pack they buy and every month they subscribe is **100% pure profit**.
*   **Zero API Liability**: Your heaviest "Power Users" move their high-frequency token costs onto their own credit cards, while still paying you for the privilege of using your interface and prompt engineering.

## 2. Economic Breakdown (Per Action)

| User Type | API Cost to Owner | Ink Cost to User | Owner Profit Margin |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard Pro** | ~$0.005 | 10 Ink | High (95%+) |
| **BYO-API Pro** | **$0.000** | **5 Ink** (50% Disc) | **100% (Infinite)** |

## 3. User Experience
*   **The Pitch**: "The fuel is yours, the engine is ours."
*   **Secure Storage**: The API key is stored only in the user's local browser (`localStorage`). It is never saved to your database, reducing your security liability.
*   **Incentive**: Users are happy to bring their own key because it makes their "Ink balance" last twice as long.

## 4. Marketing Angle
Market this as "Studio Autonomy." Professional artists like knowing exactly what they are paying for. "Use our professional models by default, or connect your own Studio key for absolute control and 50% lower Ink usage."
