## Script

### Intro (0:00–0:20)

> If you’re stuck on the **next bar**, not the whole song — this is built for that.  
> Bar 4 Bar is a **bar-by-bar** rhyme book and co-writer. Not “write me a hit in one click.”  
> I’ll paste a messy verse, split it into structure, then generate **one line** that actually rhymes.

**On screen:** Vault home → tap into a track or create album/track.

---

### Paste lyrics (0:20–1:10)

> Most of us have notes in random order. I paste everything into the editor.

**Actions:**

1. Open track editor
2. Paste 8–16 lines of rough text (or use your own)
3. Point out it’s still messy

> Watch this — **Smart Structure** (or paste flow) splits hooks and verses.

**Actions:**

4. Run Smart Structure / paste with section tags
5. Show `[Verse]` / `[Hook]` sections in the left panel

**Line:** “Your notes app isn’t a rhyme book. This is.”

---

### Next bar + rhymes (1:10–2:30)

> I’m stuck on bar five. **Rhyme Pulse** shows the pattern on the last word.

**Actions:**

1. Tap a bar → Rhyme Pulse
2. Show highlights / rhyme groups briefly
3. Tap **Generate Next Line** (or Co-Writer equivalent)
4. Accept one line — show it fits the rhyme scheme

> It suggests the **next bar**, not a whole new song. You stay in control.

**Optional 10s:** DNA / vibe on album — “same world across tracks” (see [TIKTOK_STRATEGY.md](./TIKTOK_STRATEGY.md)).

---

### Free vs Pro (2:30–3:30) — keep short

> Free tier: **250 Ink** at signup, real workstation — one album, two tracks.  
> Pro is **$7.77 a month** — unlimited projects, full co-writer, Performance Mode, Freestyle.  
> Link below — no download required; works in your phone browser. Add to Home Screen for full-screen.

**On screen:** Quick flash Usage page — do not linger on pricing more than 45s.

---

### Phone / PWA (3:30–4:20)

> On iPhone: Share → **Add to Home Screen**. On Android: Install app / Add to home.  
> Looks like a native app — it’s the same studio.

**On screen:** 5s phone capture of icon on home screen → open full-screen.

---

### Outro (4:20–end)

> Link in description — free demo. If you’re serious about bars, try one verse tonight.  
> Comment what you want the next tutorial to cover: Freestyle pad, DNA Drop, or Performance Mode.
